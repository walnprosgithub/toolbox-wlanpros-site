Field & Trade Reference plates

The built environment a wireless install actually lands in: enclosures, classified areas, code, safety, site access and the paperwork that gates a job. Each plate also has a live screen inside the Toolbox app.

Read the sizes before you print. Thirteen of these are drawing-sized rather than document-sized, because they are meant to go on a wall beside the plan set rather than into a folder.

ARCH C, 18 × 24 in, holds every one of them — rotate the LED Decoder 90°, as it is the one plate wider than it is tall. A2 will not do it: at 16.54 in wide it is just under an inch too narrow for the 17.5 in plates, so in metric you need A1.

No plotter? Scale-to-fit on Letter or A4 stays readable on a clipboard, but the type gets small. Throughput Testing is already US Letter and prints anywhere.
