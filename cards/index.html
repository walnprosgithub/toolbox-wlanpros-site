Why these were reissued

The Free Space Path Loss at 1 m row on the earlier 5 GHz and 6 GHz cards was about 1 dB short. It showed less loss than the formula gives. At 5.180 GHz it read 45.74 dB where the answer is 46.73 dB. One character in one formula.

The 2.4 GHz card was correct all along, which is exactly what made this hard to see: two tabs disagreeing with a third, in a workbook where everything looked internally consistent.

A constant offset breaks nothing you would think to check. Every channel-to-channel difference was right. The shape of the curve was right. Every comparison between two frequencies was right. The only way to catch it was to derive the number independently, and almost nobody does that to a spreadsheet they have used for years.

If you printed an earlier 5 GHz or 6 GHz card, replace it. If you built your own sheet from one, check it against the formula rather than against itself.
